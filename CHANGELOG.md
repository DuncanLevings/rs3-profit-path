# Changelog

## 1.0.0 - 2026-11-05

- First release, with everything from the 0.8 development builds below.

## 0.8 dev - 2026-10-29 to 2026-11-05

- Full catalogue: every current RuneScape Wiki money making guide (674 activities), including daily and weekly runs counted per run.
- Live profit for every activity from GE prices, with creation costs for untradeable potions and pouches, and price-based choices (shop runs, potion doses) made from today's prices.
- Requirements checked against your account: skills, combat level, quests, relics and other unlocks, achievements, items, outfits, tool belt and tiered gear. A closed bank counts as it was last seen open.
- Recommend for me: pick PvM, Skilling, Production or Recurring and get one suggestion at a time, with Show another and Don't recommend.
- PvM difficulty on a 1-10 scale, with a PvM comfort setting for recommendations.
- My account page: your character's profile, recommendation settings and hidden activities. Settings are kept per character.
- Custom activities: create your own from a monster's drops, a thieving target or from scratch, or duplicate a bundled one to edit. They show in Browse and Recommend for me, and can be shared as a line of text with Export and Import.
- Each activity page has Income, Requirements, How to do it and Getting there tabs, with Wiki warnings shown for risky activities.
- Smaller install and less lag while the window is open.

## 0.7 dev - 2026-10-29

- Prices use the last hour's average GE trades, so a single dumped or misclicked trade no longer skews profit.
- Item, quest and equipment requirements read from your backpack, worn gear, bank and tool belt, with owned or missing borders on items.
- Negative ROI is shown in red.

## 0.1 - 0.6 dev

- First versions: a resizable window opened from the Plugins ribbon, Browse with search, sorting and filters, favourites, and activity pages with an editable rate you can save or reset.
