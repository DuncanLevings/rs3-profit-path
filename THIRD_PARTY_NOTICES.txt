Profit Path: third-party notices

Profit Path's code is written independently. The bundled reference data in data/
is derived from the sources below. The editable sources, with their page revisions,
retrieval dates and hashes, are kept in catalogue-source/ (not shipped).

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
RuneScape Wiki
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

RuneScape Wiki contributors
https://runescape.wiki/
https://runescape.wiki/w/RuneScape:Copyrights
Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike 3.0 Unported
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/3.0/

Derived from the Wiki (Bucket and MediaWiki APIs, retrieved 2026-10-28 to
2026-11-04 with the User-Agent "ProfitPath-development (Discord: Tatsuya)"):

- Money making guides: every current guide's income, requirements and
  {{MMGWarning}} warnings, pinned to each page's revision. Guides the Wiki marks
  as obsolete are left out.
  https://runescape.wiki/w/Bucket:Money_making_guide
- Guide summaries and travel directions for the How to do it and Getting there
  tabs, from the same revisions and from area pages. Complete sentences were
  selected, shortened and reworded; no full strategy guides or images are copied.
- Item, outfit and equipment data: item IDs, outfit pieces, gear tiers and use
  levels (e.g. https://runescape.wiki/w/Hatchets?oldid=37191363).
  https://runescape.wiki/w/Bucket:Infobox_item
- Creation-cost recipes for untradeable Herblore and Summoning supplies.
  https://runescape.wiki/w/Bucket:Recipe
- Grand Exchange item names and thieving loot (pickpocket NPCs and stalls), for
  the custom activity editor.
  https://runescape.wiki/w/Bucket:Dropsline
- Spell, prayer and achievement requirements used by unlock checks.

Monster drop tables come from the Wiki-derived snapshot bundled in the author's
Monster Lookup plugin (built 2026-10-22), which doesn't record per-page revisions:
https://github.com/DuncanLevings/rs3-monster-lookup
Profit Path keeps each monster's drop sections, rates, quantities, roll counts and
notes, and leaves out stats, locations and prices. It is not a claim of current
Wiki data.

Changes made by Profit Path: data is normalised and packed into Lua tables; Wiki
markup is reduced to plain text; and some guide figures are recalculated, for
example supplies priced from live GE prices or their recipe, and Abyssal demon
salvage valued at its high-alchemy return. Profit uses GE Prices Library quotes,
not the Wiki's prices. The calculator follows the Wiki's Mmgtable conventions
(per-hour and per-kill inputs, output tax rounded per item, ROI on cost) but is
independently written:
https://runescape.wiki/w/Module:Mmgtable?oldid=37167449
